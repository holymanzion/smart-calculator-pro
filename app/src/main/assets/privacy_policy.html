Privacy Policy

Smart Calculator Pro · Last updated 19 September 2026

In short: Smart Calculator Pro collects nothing about you. The only thing it ever sends is a request for published exchange rates, and only when you use the Currency tool. Nothing you type is ever transmitted.

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how the Android application Smart Calculator Pro (package com.holymanzion.calculator, "the app") handles information. The app is published by an independent developer, contactable at the address at the end of this page.

2. Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, it does not gather:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • your location, contacts, photos, files or calendar;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics;
  • crash reports or diagnostic telemetry.

3. The one network request the app makes

INTERNET is the only permission the app requests, and it exists for a single feature: the Currency tool, which downloads published exchange rates.

What that request does and does not involve:

  • It is a plain GET for a public list of rates from exchangerate-api.com. It carries no account, no identifier and nothing you have typed.
  • The amounts and currencies you convert are never sent. The whole rate table is downloaded and the arithmetic happens on your device, so the provider cannot see what you converted or how much.
  • As with any internet request, the provider's servers necessarily see your IP address and the fact that a request was made. That is inherent to how the internet works, and is governed by their terms.
  • Rates are cached on your device and refreshed at most once every twelve hours, so the request is infrequent.
  • Every other tool works with no connection at all. The calculator, converter, discount, bill, loan, percentage, date and BMI tools never touch the network. The Currency tool also has a manual-rate mode you can use entirely offline.

4. What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it behaves sensibly between sessions:

  • Calculation history — the most recent 100 calculations;
  • Preferences — your theme, angle mode, keypad and formatting choices.

This is held in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read. It never leaves your device through any action of this app. Clearing history in Settings, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

5. Android backup

If you have Android's system backup switched on, Android may copy the app's settings and history to your own Google account as part of routine device backup. That transfer is performed by Android, not by this app, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

6. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no social media integration and no third-party trackers of any kind.

There is exactly one external service: the exchange rate provider described in section 3, contacted only by the Currency tool and only to download public rates. It receives no personal data from the app.

7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information whatsoever, it collects none from children either, and complies with COPPA and similar rules by simply not gathering data.

8. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data a company holds about you. No personal data about you is held, so there is nothing to disclose or erase on request. The locally stored history and settings described in section 4 are already entirely under your control.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised before that version is released. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact
